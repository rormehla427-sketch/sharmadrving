Frequently Asked

What vehicles are used for training? +

We maintain a fleet of modern luxury vehicles, primarily consisting of late-model sedans and SUVs from premium German manufacturers, all equipped with dual-control safety systems.

Do you offer door-step pickup? +

Yes, we offer complimentary pick-up and drop-off services for our students within a 15km radius of our training centers.

Can I choose my instructor? +

Absolutely. If you have a preference based on recommendations or past experiences, we will do our utmost to accommodate your request, subject to availability.

How many sessions are recommended? +

We offer two streamlined courses: a 12-day manual car training and a 10-day automatic car training. Both are perfectly designed to cover all essentials from basic controls to real-world traffic.
